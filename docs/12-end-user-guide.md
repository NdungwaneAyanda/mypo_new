# End-User Guide

## For Suppliers (Applicants)

### 1. Create an Account

1. Go to the website and click **Get Started** or navigate to `/auth`.
2. Switch to the **Register** tab.
3. Enter your email address and a strong password.
4. Click **Register** — you are logged in immediately and assigned a unique supplier reference code (e.g. `SUP-00001`).

---

### 2. Submit a Funding Application

1. Log in and go to your **Dashboard**, then click **Apply for Funding** — or navigate directly to `/apply`.
2. Fill in the application form:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Your registered business name |
| Contact Name | Full name of the contact person |
| Email | Contact email for this application |
| Phone | Contact number |
| Industry | The sector of the PO (e.g. Construction, Retail) |
| PO Amount | Total value of the purchase order |
| Cost of Delivery | What it will cost you to fulfil the PO |
| Amount Needed | The funding you are requesting |
| Customer Name | Name of the company that issued the PO |
| Payment Terms | When the customer will pay (e.g. 30 days, 60 days) |
| Description | (Optional) Any additional context |

3. Click **Submit**. You will receive a confirmation email with your application reference code (e.g. `APP-00042`).

---

### 3. Upload Supporting Documents

All 6 documents below are **required** — you cannot submit the application until every one is attached. Accepted formats: `.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx` (max **5 MB** each).

| Document | What to upload |
|---|---|
| **Purchase Order** | The actual PO document issued to your company |
| **Company Registration Document** | CIPC registration certificate for your business |
| **Bank Confirmation Letter** | Letter from your bank confirming your account details |
| **Director ID** | A copy of the director's South African ID or passport |
| **Company Proof of Address** | Recent utility bill or bank statement showing your business address |
| **Director Proof of Address** | Recent utility bill or bank statement showing the director's home address |

Click each document box in the form to browse and select the file. A green tick appears when a file is attached. Once all 6 are attached, the **Submit** button becomes active.

---

### 4. Track Your Application

On your **Dashboard**, you can see all your applications and their current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your application is live and visible to funders |
| **Reviewed** | A funder has claimed it and is reviewing the details |
| **Successful** | A funder has agreed to fund your application — congratulations! |
| **Declined** | The application was not approved |

When your application becomes **Successful**, you will receive an email and the chat feature becomes available.

---

### 5. Chat with Your Funder

Once your application is **Successful**, a **Chat** section appears on your application in the Dashboard.

- Send messages directly to your assigned funder.
- Messages are delivered in real time — no need to refresh.
- All messages are saved and visible when you return to the application.

---

### 6. Update Your Profile

Go to `/profile` to update your company name, contact name, and phone number. Your email and reference code cannot be changed from the profile page.

---

### 7. Forgot Your Password

1. Go to `/forgot-password`.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for **1 hour**).
4. Click the link and enter your new password.

---

## For Funders

### 1. Register as a Funder

Navigate to `/register-funder` and complete the signup form:

- Company details (name, contact, email, phone, website)
- Years in business
- Funding capacity range (e.g. `R500k - R2m`)
- Industries you fund
- Minimum and maximum PO amounts you consider

You will receive a funder reference code (e.g. `FND-00001`) and be logged in automatically.

---

### 2. Browse Opportunities

After logging in, your **Dashboard** shows all available funding applications with status `pending` or `reviewed`. Each card shows the company name, industry, amount needed, and submission date.

You will also receive **real-time notifications** when new applications are submitted — no need to refresh the page.

---

### 3. Claim an Application

When you find a suitable application, you have two options:

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Review** | Marks the application as `reviewed` and assigns it to you for due diligence. Other funders can still see it. |
| **Fund** | Marks the application as `successful`, assigns it to you, and notifies the supplier by email. Chat becomes available. |

---

### 4. Chat with the Supplier

Once you fund an application (status `successful`), a **Chat** section appears. Use it to coordinate directly with the supplier regarding documents, timelines, and payment.

---

### 5. Unsubscribe from Notifications

If you no longer wish to receive new opportunity emails, use the unsubscribe link included in every opportunity notification email. This sets your account to inactive and stops email notifications. You can be reactivated by an admin.
